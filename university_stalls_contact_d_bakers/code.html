How much notice is required for custom cakes?

We recommend a 24 to 48-hour prior notice for bespoke tiered designs, fondant figurines, and custom flavours. However, classic brownies, tea cakes, and cupcakes can often be readied same-day if placed before 1:00 PM!

account_balance_wallet
What payment methods do you accept?

We accept instant UPI transfers (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, QR code), Net Banking, and Cash on Pick-up at our Model Town counter. A 50% deposit confirms customized festival hampers & milestone cakes.

verified
Are the bakes 100% vegetarian / eggless?

Yes! 100% pure vegetarian (eggless) options are crafted in dedicated sanitised zones with zero cross-contamination. We use pure dairy butter, premium cocoa, and unadulterated flours without artificial stabilizers.
