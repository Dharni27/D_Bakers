## Brand & Style

This design system embodies the warmth, craftsmanship, and bespoke luxury of a boutique neighborhood home bakery. Rooted in tactile indulgence, it blends classic European confectionery aesthetics with an approachable, intimate charm. 

### Design Movement: Tactile Minimalist Romance
The visual language merges soft minimalism with delicate, sensory warmth. It eschews sharp corporate digital cues in favor of pillowy geometries, gentle textural contrast, and generously aerated layouts reminiscent of fine culinary editorial spreads.

### Target Audience & Emotional Arc
- **Audience:** Discerning local patrons, celebratory cake shoppers, gourmet dessert lovers, and gift-givers seeking thoughtful, small-batch, handcrafted delicacies.
- **Emotional Response:** Comfort, sensory delight, trust in ingredient purity, and an immediate craving for fresh bakes. The interface feels like stepping into a sunlit morning pastry atelier scented with vanilla bean and caramelized butter.

## Colors

The palette is derived from classic pastry essentials: Tahitian vanilla cream, berry glazes, toasted butter, and deep single-origin cacao.

- **Primary (`#3D2314`):** Rich Dark Cocoa. Provides high-contrast, grounding legibility for typography, structural outlines, and decisive CTA states without the harshness of pure black.
- **Secondary (`#E57399`):** Strawberry Rose. Imparts a soft, artisanal blush tone used for accent badges, delicate borders, interactive selections, and celebratory iconography. Tinted variations (`#F4A7B9`) are reserved for soft fills.
- **Tertiary (`#D97706`):** Toasted Butter Amber. Used sparingly for highlights, star ratings, seasonal tags, and warm focal sparks.
- **Neutral (`#FDFBF7`):** Warm Vanilla Cream. The base canvas color, supplemented by `#FFF8F0` (Pastry Cloud) for layered cards and modal surfaces, eliminating digital sterility.
- **Muted Cocoa (`#7A5843`):** Secondary typographic tone for supportive metadata, subheadings, and understated captions.

## Typography

The typographic pairing reflects high-end culinary editorial design:

- **Headlines (Playfair Display):** Evokes the historic legacy of artisanal European patisseries, high-end cookbooks, and bespoke stationery. It brings natural elegance and personal touch to signature cake titles and story sections.
- **Body & Functional UI (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Offers balanced, open letterforms that balance the ornamental serif headlines. Its rounded geometric construction guarantees high legibility for ingredient lists, weight/serving sizes, price tags, and checkout sequences.
- **Editorial Italic Flashes:** Playfair Display Italic should be used selectively for evocative descriptors (e.g., *“Freshly Hand-piped”*, *“Belgian Couverture”*) to inject bespoke personality.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy uses a disciplined fluid grid grounded in open, generous negative space to evoke an uncluttered, premium showcase.

### Grid & Structure
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12-column layout with `3rem` margins and `1.5rem` gutters. Maximum content canvas bounded at `1280px` for optimal reading intimacy.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `2rem` margins and `1.25rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `1.25rem` margins and `1rem` gutters.

### Rhythm & Reflow
- Culinary showcase cards reflow from 3 or 4 columns on desktop down to 2 columns on tablet, and single full-width cards or smooth horizontal snap carousels on mobile.
- Vertical pacing relies on alternating large macro-margins (`space-xl`) between narrative sections to prevent dense visual clutter and let product imagery breathe.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids cold synthetic drop shadows in favor of ambient warm radiance and soft tactile layering.

### Surface Tiers
- **Canvas Base:** `#FDFBF7` (Vanilla Base).
- **Raised Containers (Cards, Sheets):** `#FFF8F0` (Clotted Cream) paired with faint `1px` borders in `#F4A7B9` at 20% opacity.
- **Elevated Modals & Menus:** `#FFFFFF` resting atop blurred, warm-tinted backdrop underlays (`rgba(61, 35, 20, 0.25)`).

### Ambient Warm Shadows
- **Card Rest:** `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(61, 35, 20, 0.04), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(61, 35, 20, 0.02)`
- **Card Hover / Floating:** `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(61, 35, 20, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(61, 35, 20, 0.04)`
- **Interactive Buttons:** `0 4px 14px rgba(229, 115, 153, 0.28)` for secondary action emphasis, creating a diffused, confectionery glow.

## Shapes

The shape system draws inspiration from natural baked forms—pillowy brioche, smooth macarons, and sculpted chocolate truffles.

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem`):** Applied to form inputs, small badges, and tooltips.
- **Medium Radius (`1rem`):** Standard for product showcase cards, order summaries, and modal dialogs.
- **Large Radius (`1.5rem`):** Hero media containers, highlight banners, and promotional wrappers.
- **Pill Shapes:** Applied universally to buttons, chips, cart counters, and dietary badges (e.g., "100% Eggless", "Made with Butter") to maximize friendly touch affordance.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Filled in Dark Cocoa (`#3D2314`) with Vanilla Cream text (`#FDFBF7`), fully rounded pill shape, bold tracking (`label-lg`). Hover shifts softly to `#2A180E` with a slight upward translation (`translateY(-1px)`).
- **Secondary / Blush:** Filled with Strawberry Rose (`#E57399`) and white text. Used for immediate ordering or celebration cake customization actions.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Bordered in `#3D2314` (1.5px thickness) over transparent fill, maintaining high tactile craftsmanship.

### Chips & Dietary Badges
- Compact pill-shaped tags. 
- "Eggless" or "Signature" tags utilize a soft cream base (`#FFF8F0`), a gentle border (`#F4A7B9`), and Dark Cocoa text, accompanied by mini dot indicators in Toasted Amber (`#D97706`).

### Cards (Product & Flavour Profiles)
- Clotted Cream backgrounds (`#FFF8F0`) with a 1px border (`rgba(61, 35, 20, 0.06)`).
- Generous padding (`space-md` to `space-lg`). Images feature a softly rounded frame (`rounded-lg`) with an inner warm vignette to seamlessly blend bakery food photography into the cream background.

### Input Fields
- Generously padded (`14px 18px`), rounded at `0.75rem`.
- Vanilla background with 1px border in `#3D2314` at 15% opacity.
- Focus state reveals a 1.5px border in Strawberry Rose (`#E57399`) with a soft rose ambient focus ring (`0 0 0 3px rgba(229, 115, 153, 0.15)`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Custom circular and squircle controls in Dark Cocoa. Checked state features a warm Rose or Cocoa fill with a crisp Vanilla checkmark/dot.

### Domain-Specific Components
- **Flavour & Weight Selector:** Segmented pill controls allowing seamless toggling between cake weights (e.g., "0.5 kg", "1.0 kg") and custom eggless options.
- **Baker's Note Ribbon:** A soft-edged callout card featuring subtle dashed borders evoking bakery string and parchment paper, used for custom piping messages and delivery instructions.
